| Question (as asked) | Answer | Carried out as |
|---|---|---|
| **1** — The "Customer Payment Page" button (settings) and its tutorial step now open the no-board notice: remove, point at a board's page, or leave? | The model, restated: a standard per-sale payment link without a board; a board is one QR the merchant generates and prints for real-life scanning; "they can make them as much as they want"; the QR "never changes once made until deleted". | Confirms the rework's model; nothing to change. Two things go back to the owner: the button question was not answered, so the button stays as it is; and a business can have **at most 10 boards at a time** (`TAPT_STONE_LIMIT`, `server/storage.ts:450`, since `efb456f7`), not "as much as they want". A board's QR is its board's fixed address (`/pay/<business>/stone/<board>`), unchanged until the board is deleted. |
| **2** — A "new link" button to re-share a board-less sale after a reload? | "no", but a dropdown at the top of the blue section of the terminal's share page to choose which sale to share. After sending, the terminal goes to the share page showing the sale just sent; coming back shows the latest sale until another is chosen. | The phone terminal's share page gets that dropdown, listing the open sales it can share: a board sale shares its board's page; a board-less sale its own link, which the phone that made the sale remembers (on that phone, until the sale is paid or cancelled). Sending a sale opens the share page with it chosen; a sale chosen by hand stays chosen until another sale is sent or it is paid. The September pop-up that showed a new sale's link is replaced by the share page, which it would otherwise cover. The share page shows the sale's real QR code rather than a decorative one. |
| **3** — Two pre-existing faults: "download QR" saves an unscannable picture; the cash-sale "copy receipt link" copies the demo address. | "fix them" | "download QR" saves a real, scannable PNG of exactly the link being shared. The receipt link's cause was deeper: **the phone never recorded a cash sale at all** (no action was wired, so "success" showed and nothing was saved). A cash sale is now recorded, the success screen shows what was recorded, and its receipt link is that sale's receipt. |
