## S6 starting facts (read, not yet changed)

S6 is R1-T7's last batch: the private upload lifecycle. The owner's decisions of
2026-09-29 are not to be asked again: scan invoice documents with ClamAV and keep a
document from every reader until the scan says it is clean; delete an upload no
invoice uses 12 months after it was uploaded; attached documents follow the 7-year
rule of 2026-09-21 and are not touched.

Where documents are written and read today (`server/routes.ts`, `server/storage.ts`):

- Written once: `POST /api/property/invoices/document` (also used by the trades
  screens), after the type and magic-byte checks, through `saveUploadedFile`.
- Read by: `GET /api/invoice-documents/:name` (the business, and the audited admin
  branch), `GET /api/checkout/document/:token` (the invoice's payer),
  `invoiceHasCheckoutDocument` (whether the payment page shows a link), and the
  attach checks (`requireOwnedInvoiceDocument` in the routes, `holdOwnedInvoiceDocument`
  and its property twin in storage).
- Referenced by: property invoices, trades quotes and trades invoices (`documentUrl`).
- Logos are a separate public folder and are not part of the scanning decision.

What S6 has to settle in its preflight, with the conservative default in brackets:

1. Where a scan's verdict is kept [a new table keyed by the file, so the migration is
   additive like 0030 and 0031, rather than new columns on `uploaded_files`].
2. When the scan runs [at upload, before anything is stored: an infected upload is
   refused and never stored; a scanner that cannot be reached refuses the upload].
3. Documents uploaded before S6 [no verdict, so kept from every reader until a scan
   pass gives one].
4. The scanner when it is not configured [uploads and document reads refuse; this is
   what "kept from every reader until clean" means, and it makes ClamAV a deployment
   prerequisite, in development too]. **This one is the owner's to know about before
   S6 ships:** without ClamAV running, attaching a document stops working.
5. The 12-month deletion [a scheduler pass; "uses" means any property invoice,
   trades quote or trades invoice naming the document].

ClamAV is not installed in this workspace (`clamscan`, `clamd` not found), so the
real scanner can only be exercised against a stand-in here.

A new migration also needs: an entry in `server/migration-baseline-contract.ts`, an
entry in `REAL_MIGRATIONS` (`server/__tests__/migrate.test.ts`), the table in
`shared/schema.ts`, and a regenerated empty-database fingerprint with `RECORDED:`
repointed in `.github/workflows/verify.yml` (see the project memory on the local
