# T-3 — a later approval in a session supersedes its recorded decline (2026-10-15)

The owner decided to build full supersession now
([decision](../../../decisions/2026-10-15-t3-build-full-supersession-owner-decision.md)). Design:
[ADR revision 3](../../../decisions/2026-10-15-t3-decline-supersession.md), refused in
[pass 1](T3-review-pass-1-2026-10-15.md) and approved with conditions in
[pass 2](T3-review-pass-2-2026-10-15.md). The implementation was approved with conditions in
[pass 3](T3-review-pass-3-2026-10-15.md); all are resolved. Applied to no shared database; nothing
deployed.

## What changed

- **Migration 0050** (applied nowhere; [owner runbook](../../../operations/runbook-2026-10-15-apply-0049-0050.md)):
  - settlement events carry their card's transaction ID (backfilled) and the decline an approval
    supersedes;
  - at most one declined and one final event per session, in order, and append-only;
  - the registry holds one supersession claim per session;
  - a binding's transaction changes only by a supersession, checked at commit;
  - terminal attempts are frozen except `declined` → `approved`;
  - a retail attempt's transaction ID changes only then.

  Objects replacing earlier ones keep their names, so every earlier migration's baseline
  contract still holds (tested). 0049 was revised the same way, before being applied anywhere.
- **Finalizers** (retail `DatabaseStorage`, invoice `DurablePaymentStorage`), reconciliation only:
  - supersession is a compare-and-set from `declined`, after a registry check;
  - a repeated or stale decline is `reused`;
  - a second attempt's decline after its target was paid is recorded on that attempt alone;
  - every database refusal maps to `conflict`, so the worker's `PAYMENT_APPROVAL_NOT_RECORDED`
    alert fires;
  - a superseded decline's undelivered notifications are retired, or skipped at preparation.
- **Merchant pre-change reconcile** asks about every live session first, then decline-only sessions.
- **MemStorage** refuses reconciliation explicitly, as before; supersession is PostgreSQL-only.
- The transition graph documents `supersedeDecline`.
