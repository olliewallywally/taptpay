# Billing 402s — one message, from the banner (R1-T9, 2026-09-23)

Date: 2026-09-23 UTC. Branch: `remediation/r1-continuation-20260907`. Commits: `1f6a8673` (one
billing error and the banner's words), `8a4e8c20` (desktop screens), `09b07a43` (the customer's
quote page), `a2dee290` (phone screens). Plan: R1-T9, v2.2 §8.8 (full plan PDF p. 29): "Make
billing `402` errors state the required billing action without conflicting duplicate banners."

## In plain words

When a business's TaptPay subscription needs attention, the server refuses to send payments for
it (HTTP 402). The app used to handle that three wrong ways:

- **It said it twice, in two different ways.** A banner said "Credit or debit card required" (a
  rule the server dropped on 2026-08-10) and the button's own message said "Your subscription
  needs attention…".
- **It said something untrue.** Tap to Pay said "Payment Declined — Please try again", but no card
  was declined: the server refuses before charging anything.
- **It said nothing about billing.** Resending a link said "Could not resend link"; resending to
  several tenants said "Resent 0 · 1 failed". Neither said why.

Now one banner says it: **"Subscription needs attention — You can't send payments until it's
sorted in Billing"**, with **Open Billing**. The button adds nothing, and what was typed stays, so
it can be sent again once billing is sorted. A customer accepting a quote from such a business is
told to contact the business, instead of being shown the business's own billing message.

## What the server does

- `requireBillingCard` (`server/routes.ts:120`) answers 402 with
  `{ code: "BILLING_CARD_REQUIRED", message: "Your subscription needs attention before you can
  send payments. Open Billing in Settings." }` when `subscriptionHasPaidAccess` is false
  (`server/billing-card.ts`). Since `bee2be18` (2026-08-10) that is about paid access, card or not.
- It guards 15 routes: `POST /api/transactions`, `/api/transactions/cash-sale`,
  `/api/transactions/tap-to-pay`, `/api/merchants/:merchantId/nfc-pay`, `/api/v1/transactions`,
  `/api/property/tenants/:tenantId/schedules`, `/api/property/invoices`,
  `/api/property/invoices/:id/resend`, `/api/trades/quotes`, `/api/trades/quotes/:id/resend`,
  `/api/trades/quotes/token/:token/respond` (a customer accepting), `/api/trades/invoices`,
  `/api/trades/invoices/:id/resend`, `/api/trades/invoices/:id/send-balance`,
  `/api/trades/schedules`. No GET answers 402.

## The shared layer — `1f6a8673`

- `apiRequest`, `tradesFetch` and `propFetch` raise the banner and throw one
  `BillingCardRequiredError` on a 402, so an action can tell it apart (`isBillingCardRequired`)
  and add nothing. Pages that call `fetch` directly raise the banner with
  `notifyIfBillingCardRequired` and throw the same error.
- The banner (`components/notification-system.tsx`) says what the server means, once, however many
  402s arrive (sticky notifications are de-duplicated), with Open Billing
  (`/settings?section=billing`, where it already went).

## Found on the way (not changed)

**On a phone, rent automations cannot be paused, resumed or cancelled, and batch resend cannot be
reached.** Both live on the phone property terminal's "batch send / schedules" screen, and nothing
opens it: `7b99299a` (2026-06-02) replaced the subbar's "batch" entry with "bill", and no button,
link or dashboard deep link leads there since. Tablet and desktop manage schedules on their own
terminal. For the owner (or R8): give the screen an entry again, or remove it.

## Decisions made here (the owner may overrule)

- The banner's words: "Subscription needs attention — You can't send payments until it's sorted in
  Billing", replacing "Credit or debit card required — Please enter a valid credit or debit card in
  Settings before sending payments". The old words describe a rule the server dropped on
  2026-08-10.
- The customer's words: "This quote can't be accepted online right now. Please contact the
  business to go ahead."
- Tap to Pay closes its overlay on a billing refusal rather than gaining a new "billing" state.

## Open for the owner

1. **A customer who accepts a quote while the business's billing needs attention is turned away,
   and the business is never told.** It is a customer ready to go ahead. Should the business hear
   about it (an email, or a notice when it next signs in)?
2. **The customer's browser still receives the business's billing message.** The quote page no
   longer shows it, but the server's 402 reply to the public quote link carries "Your subscription
   needs attention… Open Billing in Settings." Recommended: the server gives the customer wording
   on that public route too (a small server change, not made here).
