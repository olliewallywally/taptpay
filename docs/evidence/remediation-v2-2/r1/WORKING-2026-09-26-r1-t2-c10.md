## 1 — split-invoice share sessions: design (2026-09-26, before code)

Found while designing: the checkout page opens several provider sessions per payer (an Apple Pay
and a Google Pay pre-session on load, and a card session on submit), and most are never used. So a
session cannot reserve a share: a payer's unused pre-sessions would hold shares nobody pays. Also,
MemStorage's split-share claims return null (`atomicClaimSplitShare`, `atomicClaimJobSplitShare`),
so no harness test has ever paid a split share; and the rent/trades notifications find invoices only
by the pinned session, which split invoices never set, so a split share closed in the browser before
completing was never recorded.

Design:
- Migration 0030: table `invoice_split_sessions` — the provider session id (primary key), exactly
  one of `rent_invoice_id` / `job_invoice_id` (foreign keys), `amount_cents` (> 0), `payer_email`,
  `opened_at`, `paid_at`. Declared in `shared/schema.ts`; contract and `REAL_MIGRATIONS` entries; a
  new dated empty-database fingerprint for CI.
- Every session opened for a split invoice is recorded with its amount and the email given with it
  (no longer appended to `split_payer_emails`).
- A split completion (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and the notification) accepts only a session
  recorded for that invoice: anything else is 403 before the provider is asked. A recorded session
  still pays at most one share (the existing `split_paid_sessions` dedup), and its row is marked paid.
- The split count locks once any session has been recorded (409), not only once a share is paid.
- The rent GST copies go to the tenant, the co-tenants and the emails of sessions that paid, not to
  every address anyone typed.
- The notifications also find a split share by its recorded session.
- Left for R3 (payment attempts engine), recorded: amounts are still fixed per session when it is
  opened (the last share pays the remainder), so sessions opened at once can leave up to N−1 cents
  unpaid; a session that completes after the shares are all paid is charged and not recorded
  (pre-existing; now logged as an event for refund).
- Applying 0030 to the development database is the owner's (the safety check denies such writes);
  until then the split checkout fails in dev only. Production is closed.
