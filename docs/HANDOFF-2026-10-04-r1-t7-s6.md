## Added later the same session: R1-T5a (`8b026aa7`, harness fix `01e22c4b`)

Sign in with Apple is started. Plan, decomposition (T5a–T5e) and the T5a preflight:
`docs/PLAN-2026-10-04-r1-t5-sign-in-with-apple.md`. T5a is `server/apple-sign-in.ts`
(the client secret, the code exchange, identity-token verification, Apple's key set)
and the four `APPLE_*` settings. Nothing calls it yet.

**T5b starting facts** (read, not yet changed; write its §21.1 preflight first):

- Apple answers with a **cross-site form POST** from `https://appleid.apple.com`. It
  must be listed in `CROSS_SITE_CALLBACKS` (`server/cross-site.ts`) with that exact
  origin; `express.urlencoded` is already global, so the form is parsed.
- The starting cookie must be `SameSite=None; Secure` to come back on that POST
  (Google's is `Lax` because Google returns by a top-level GET). So the flow works
  only on https, which Apple requires of the redirect address anyway. The session
  cookie is not sent on a cross-site POST: T5c's "connect from Settings" has to carry
  its intent in the starting cookie, under the server's signature.
- The state is taken up once exactly as Google's is (`takeAuthThrottleSlot` on a
  bucket named by the state), and callbacks that reach Apple are counted per address.
- The handoff is reusable as it stands: `createAuthHandoffCode`,
  `consumeAuthHandoffCode`, `startBusinessSession`; the login page redeems by a
  same-origin POST.
- A new table is needed for Apple identities: issuer, client id and subject (unique
  together), the login it belongs to or pending, the email and relay flag captured on
  the first authorization. That is migration `0033`, with the contract entry,
  `REAL_MIGRATIONS` entry, schema declaration and a regenerated fingerprint
  (`convergence.sh` in the scratch folder).
- Apple's form also carries a `user` field with the person's name on the first
  authorization only. It is not signed: take the name from it, never an email.
- A first sign-in whose token carries no email (Apple sends it once) cannot make a
  business: `merchants.email` is required. The preflight must say what the person
  is told.
- No joining by email, ever (the plan). A matching email records a pending identity
  and tells the person to sign in and connect Apple from Settings.
